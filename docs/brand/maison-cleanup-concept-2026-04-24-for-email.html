What changes

  • Light paper surfaces instead of dark glassy gradients

Palette direction

Base: warm cream and soft parchment.

Type: walnut / ink, not pure black.

Accent: muted gold plus restrained teal.

Optional warmth: tiny terracotta moments, not everywhere.
