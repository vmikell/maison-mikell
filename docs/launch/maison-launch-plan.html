Maison Launch Plan

Prepared for Victor, April 10, 2026

Overview

This package turns Maison from a working household MVP into a launchable paid product with a production checklist, app-store packaging plan, pricing strategy, and a focused marketing plan.

Core recommendation: Freeze major feature expansion for now and focus the next cycle on production quality, app packaging, onboarding, billing, and positioning.

1. Production Readiness Checklist

Objective: Move Maison from "works for our household" to "safe to sell to other households."

Product reliability, must complete

  • Verify the sign-in flow on the real deployed app, especially the join-screen flash issue.
  • Audit all loading, empty, and error states across planner, calendar, shopping, admin, and join flows.
  • Add crash and error tracking, recommended: Sentry.
  • Add product analytics for core events.
  • Confirm Firestore rules cover all intended household actions safely.
  • Review reminder flows for duplicate sends, partial failures, and silent failures.
  • Add a recovery path for failed writes and offline or reconnect edge cases.
  • Make sign-out, session expiry, and membership transitions predictable.

Core analytics events

  • account_created
  • signed_in
  • household_joined
  • invite_code_used
  • task_added
  • task_completed
  • shopping_item_added
  • shopping_item_checked
  • reminder_sent

Data and operations

  • Define backup and export strategy for household data.
  • Add an internal admin and debug checklist for production incidents.
  • Remove or hide any consumer-visible debug text before launch.
  • Document Netlify deployment recovery steps.
  • Repair Git-based Netlify deployment configuration or replace it with a trusted deploy workflow.

Legal and trust

  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of service
  • Support email and support flow
  • Account deletion or request flow
  • Basic data retention policy

Product polish

  • Redesign color system and core visual language.
  • Tighten spacing, typography, and interaction consistency.
  • Improve onboarding clarity for first-time users.
  • Add a cleaner household invitation experience.
  • Review mobile responsiveness across common iPhone and Android sizes.

2. App Store Packaging Roadmap

Recommendation: Use Capacitor to wrap the existing Vite + React app for iOS and Android.

  • Fastest route to shipping on both stores
  • Preserves the current codebase
  • Supports gradual addition of native features later
  • Avoids premature full native rewrites

Target architecture

  • Frontend: existing React and Vite app
  • Backend: Firebase Auth and Firestore
  • Hosting: Netlify for web
  • Mobile shell: Capacitor

Phases

  • Prepare the web app for native wrapping.
  • Add Capacitor and generate iOS and Android projects.
  • Set up native shells, signing, icons, and device testing.
  • Prepare store assets, policy URLs, and review metadata.
  • Decide and implement billing compliance for native subscriptions.
First milestone: create a mobile implementation branch and get a working internal install on one iPhone and one Android device before store-polish work.

3. Pricing and Offer

Maison should launch as a paid product, with no free tier.

Recommended launch offer

  • Founding lifetime access: $179
  • Available for the first 14 days after public launch
  • Positioned as founding-member pricing

Recommended standard pricing

  • Monthly: $12/month
  • Yearly: $96/year

Positioning: The home operating system for couples. One calm place for maintenance, shopping, reminders, and household coordination.

4. Marketing Plan

Goal: Prove that households outside Victor and Riah's own home will pay for Maison.

Ideal first customers

  • Couples sharing a home
  • Busy homeowners
  • Households that already feel coordination friction
  • People who want calm, not hyper-optimized productivity culture

What can be executed next

  • Homepage copy and positioning refinement
  • App Store and Play Store listing copy
  • Launch FAQ and launch emails
  • Founding-offer landing page copy
  • Screenshot plan and demo script
  • Warm outreach list and community post drafts

30-day plan

  • Foundation: pricing, positioning, launch page, legal pages
  • Asset creation: screenshots, demo walkthrough, launch posts
  • Controlled launch: warm network, a few outside households, fast feedback loop
  • Expansion: broader content and outreach, testimonials, close founding offer after 14 days
