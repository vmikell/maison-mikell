# Maison Grassroots Content Batch

Prepared: 2026-04-24

## Goal

Create a real 2–3 week organic content batch that matches Maison’s current strongest brand direction.

The content should make people feel:
- recognized
- understood
- a little relieved
- curious about Maison

Not pitched at.

## Positioning guardrails

Keep Maison framed as:
- the calm way to run a shared home
- a premium but human household brand
- domestic, specific, and emotionally intelligent
- built for the invisible layer of home life

Avoid:
- sounding like a productivity app
- startup-y hooks
- overexplaining product features
- making every post feel like a launch ad

---

# Campaign structure

## Content pillars

### 1. Tiny home frictions
The small repeated things that create household drag.

### 2. Shared-home mental load
The invisible remembering, tracking, and following up that often falls unevenly on one person.

### 3. System, not character
Reframing common household tension as a coordination problem, not a personality flaw.

### 4. The Maison feeling
What a calmer, more coordinated home rhythm actually feels like.

### 5. Light product glimpses
Show Maison as relief, not as software theater.

# Suggested weekly cadence

## Instagram
- 2 carousels
- 2 reels
- stories on posting days

## TikTok
- 3 to 4 posts per week

## X / Threads
- 3 to 5 short posts per week
- founder-style replies around adjacent conversations

---

# Story prompts

Use these between posts:
- What is the most repeated household reminder in your home?
- What do you forget to buy over and over?
- What household thing always ends up living in one person’s head?
- What is one tiny home friction that causes way too much annoyance?

These are useful both for engagement and for stealing the audience’s own language.

---

# Visual direction for content

Keep content:
- warm
- domestic
- lightly editorial
- intimate
- not over-produced

Use:
- groceries
- counters
- pantries
- handwritten reminders
- entry tables
- dish soap / paper towels / filters / batteries / light household details
- shared-home scenes that feel normal, not staged to death

Avoid:
- loud trendy creator editing
- meme overload
- polished ad energy
- too many direct screen recordings
- generic productivity visuals

---

# Offer / CTA guidance

Rotate CTAs between:
- Join early access
- Follow Maison
- Founding launch is opening soon
- If this feels familiar, Maison will probably make sense to you

Do not hard-sell in every post.
Recognition first. Pull second. Conversion third.

---

# What success looks like

The first win is not huge reach.

The first win is people saying:
- this is exactly my house
- this is literally our problem
- I did not realize this was a system issue
- I want this when it launches

That is the signal we want.
