# Maison Week 1 Asset Pack

Prepared: 2026-04-24

## Goal

Turn Week 1 of the grassroots content plan into a publish-ready content pack.

This should be usable without needing another strategy round first.

Includes:
- final captions
- carousel slide copy
- post-by-post asset guidance
- story frames
- CTA variations
- platform-specific formatting guidance

## Tone guardrails

Keep Maison feeling:
- calm
- warm
- intimate
- emotionally intelligent
- premium without feeling slick

Avoid:
- startup hype
- productivity jargon
- sounding like an ad in every post
- overly polished brand-theater language

What needs to get bought.
What needs to get done.

Not to make home life more managerial.
To make it feel calmer.

- It might not be a relationship problem

### Asset guidance
