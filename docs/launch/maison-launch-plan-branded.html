Maison Launch Package

From household MVP to polished, paid product.

A focused plan to productionize Maison, package it for iPhone and Android, sharpen the product story, and launch with a premium paid offer.

Core thesis

Maison already solves a real household problem. That makes it a viable MVP. The next move is not endless feature expansion, it is production hardening, packaging, polish, pricing, and controlled launch execution.

Best packaging path Wrap the current React + Vite app with Capacitor for iOS and Android.
Recommended pricing $179 founding lifetime for 14 days, then $12 monthly or $96 yearly.
Target buyer Couples and households who want calm, shared home management.
Prepared for Victor, April 10, 2026

Production readiness

The first bar is simple: Maison has to feel dependable enough that other households can trust it with real home coordination.

Reliability checklist

  • Verify the real sign-in flow, especially the join-screen flash issue.
  • Audit loading, empty, and error states across planner, calendar, shopping, admin, and join flows.
  • Add crash and error tracking, recommended: Sentry.
  • Add analytics for core actions like join, add task, complete task, and shopping activity.
  • Review Firestore rules and reminder flows for edge cases and silent failures.
  • Make session expiry, sign-out, and membership transitions predictable.

Operational readiness

  • Define household data backup and export strategy.
  • Document deployment recovery because Netlify auto-deploy has been unreliable.
  • Remove consumer-visible debug output before launch.
  • Create a basic production incident checklist.

Trust and polish

  • Privacy policy, terms, support flow, and account deletion path.
  • Cleaner onboarding and invite experience.
  • Visual redesign pass: colors, typography, spacing, interaction consistency.
  • Responsive QA across common iPhone and Android screen sizes.

App Store packaging roadmap

The cleanest route to both app stores is to keep the current codebase and wrap it in a native shell.

Recommended path: Capacitor

  • Fastest route to ship on both stores
  • Preserves the existing React + Vite app
  • Lets native features arrive gradually: push, deep links, billing, icons, splash, and better auth handling
  • Avoids a premature rewrite into separate native apps
Phase 1, prepare the web appAudit Firebase auth in native context, remove browser-only assumptions, confirm webview behavior, and decide how Google sign-in should behave in-app.
Phase 2, add CapacitorInitialize Capacitor, define app identity, point at the Vite build output, and generate iOS and Android projects.
Phase 3, native shell setupConfigure signing, icons, launch assets, and real-device testing on one iPhone and one Android phone.
Phase 4, store-readinessPrepare screenshots, descriptions, privacy disclosures, support URLs, and review metadata.
Phase 5, billingImplement compliant native subscription handling if purchases happen inside the mobile apps.
First milestone: get a working internal install on one iPhone and one Android device before polishing store presentation.

Pricing and launch offer

Maison should launch as a paid product. The goal is premium clarity, not mass free-user accumulation.

Founding lifetime

$179

Available for the first 14 days after public launch. Positioned as founding-member access.

Monthly

$12

Flexible plan for households that want to try the product without a long commitment.

Yearly

$96

Recommended primary plan. Better value and better fit for a household operating system.

Why no free tier

  • The product is for running a real household, not casual dabbling.
  • Paid-only positioning creates better expectations and stronger perceived value.
  • A free tier adds support load and muddies early product positioning.

Positioning line

Maison should be framed as the home operating system for couples, a calm place for maintenance, shopping, reminders, and shared household planning.

Marketing plan

The first goal is not to reach everyone. It is to get the right households to say, “this genuinely makes our home life easier.”

Best first customers

  • Couples sharing a home
  • Busy homeowners
  • Households already feeling coordination friction
  • People who want calm rather than hyper-optimized productivity theater

What can be executed autonomously

  • Homepage copy and positioning
  • App Store and Play Store listing copy
  • Launch FAQ and email drafts
  • Founding-offer landing page copy
  • Screenshot and demo script planning
  • Warm outreach list and community post drafts

30-day launch sequence

  • Foundation: finalize pricing, positioning, landing page, and legal pages
  • Asset creation: screenshots, demo walkthrough, founder launch posts
  • Controlled launch: warm network first, then a small number of outside households
  • Expansion: broader content and outreach, testimonials, close the founding offer after 14 days

Metrics to watch

  • Visitor to signup rate
  • Signup to household-created rate
  • Household-created to week-one retention
  • Paid conversion rate
  • Yearly plan share
  • Refund and churn reasons
Recommended immediate order: production hardening, Capacitor packaging, then a visual redesign pass and launch asset creation.
